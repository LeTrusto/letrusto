## SECTION 2 — SCORING MODEL

### Methodology

Each opportunity is scored 1–10 across seven dimensions. Scores are then weighted and summed to produce an Overall Opportunity Score out of 10.

**Scoring dimensions and weights:**

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Affiliate monetization potential | 25% | Commission rate × recurring × customer LTV |
| Buyer intent | 20% | How close to purchase is the searcher |
| Search potential | 15% | Volume + growth trend in the category |
| Competition opportunity | 20% | Realistic chance of ranking against incumbents |
| Product price / LTV | 10% | Higher price = higher commission per conversion |
| Content opportunity | 5% | Can LeTrusto create genuinely useful editorial content |
| Global suitability | 5% | Accessible to a global English-speaking audience |

**Score interpretation:**

- 8–10: High priority — build now
- 6–7: Strong — build in next quarter
- 4–5: Moderate — build when higher-priority content is established
- 1–3: Weak — deprioritize or avoid
