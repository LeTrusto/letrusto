## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### What We Researched

~90 programs across 11 categories were audited against official provider affiliate pages. Programs were scored across:
- Affiliate availability (current, verified)
- Commission economics
- India accessibility
- Competition level
- Commercial intent
- LeTrusto architecture fit

### Key Findings

**Best digital categories for LeTrusto (ranked):**
1. **SaaS / Business Software** — High recurring commissions, strong India demand, excellent content opportunity
2. **Marketing / SEO Software** — Extremely high commissions ($100–$300/sale), high buying intent, underserved in India
3. **Hosting / Domains / Website Builders** — Proven affiliate economics, $50–$200/sale, massive India market
4. **VPN / Cybersecurity** — Strong recurring, 100%+ first-month commissions, very high India demand
5. **AI Tools** — Good programs available (ElevenLabs, Jasper, Grammarly), but most major AI tools have NO affiliate program

**Critical finding:**
> The 5 biggest AI brands (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, GitHub Copilot, Cursor) have **NO public affiliate program**. LeTrusto cannot monetize them via affiliate commissions today. These are traffic-builders, not revenue-builders.

**Recommended primary niche:** SaaS / Business Software + Marketing Tools  
**Recommended secondary niche:** Hosting + Domains  
**Recommended tertiary niche:** VPN / Cybersecurity  

**Categories to deprioritize:** Electronics, Beauty, Pets, Travel (low commissions, dominated by Amazon/Flipkart/Booking, or insufficient India purchase availability)

## PART 3 — MONEY MODEL ANALYSIS

### Commission Calculation Framework

**Formula used:**
- One-time: `Product Price × Commission %`
- Recurring (first year): `Monthly Price × Commission % × 12`
- Recurring lifetime: `Annual Price × Commission %` (ongoing)

### Verified Commission Math

#### ElevenLabs (✅ Verified)
- Starter Plan: ~$5/month × 22% × 12 = $13.20/year per referral
- Creator Plan: $22/month × 22% × 12 = $58.08/year per referral
- Pro Plan: $99/month × 22% × 12 = $261.36/year per referral
- Business Plan: varies × 11% × 12
- **Potential per Pro customer:** ~$261/year for 12 months

#### Jasper (✅ Verified)
- Creator Plan: ~$39/month × 25% × 12 = $117/year per referral
- Pro Plan: ~$59/month × 25% × 12 = $177/year per referral
- Cookie: only 14 days (short window — risk of losing credit)

#### Kit / ConvertKit (✅ Verified)
- Example: $1,200/year customer → $600 first year commission
- After 12 months at Bronze tier: +10% = $120/year recurring indefinitely
- **Potential per mid-tier customer:** $600 first year + $120/year ongoing

#### Semrush (✅ Verified)
- Semrush One sale: $300 per sale (up to $450 for Platinum tier)
- Trial: $10 bonus
- 120-day cookie window is excellent
- **Potential per sale:** $200–$450 depending on plan and tier

#### Grammarly (✅ Verified)
- Free signup: $0.20 (low value but high volume possible)
- Premium upgrade: $20 flat
- 90-day cookie
- **Potential per premium user:** $20 (one-time)
- Note: No recurring commission

#### Bluehost (✅ Verified)
- $65 per qualified sale (one-time)
- 30-day cookie
- **Potential per sale:** $65 flat

#### SiteGround (✅ Verified)
- $50–$100+ per sale (tiered by volume)
- 60-day cookie
- Weekly payouts, no minimum
- **Potential per sale:** $50 at start, scaling to $100+

#### NordVPN (✅ Verified)
- 1-month sign-up: 100% commission = ~$11.99 first month
